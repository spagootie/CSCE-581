the precision is the number of flagged transactions that are actually fraud
the recall is the amount of fraud we catch

we can see that an agressive threshold like 0.1 catches fraud pretty well at 94% but only 0.082% of flagged
transactions were actually fraud.
a lenient one like 0.9983 catches only 84% of fraud, but 64% of the time it flagged something as fraud
it was right. 0.9983 is easily the best. 
we also see that the model gets less accurate when the threshold goes above a certain point.

so for credit fraud, a really high threshold is good.
